Jewish Clock Privacy Policy for Apple Platforms

Effective August 24, 2026

Jewish Clock does not track you, sell personal information, or use advertising identifiers.

Features processed on your device

  • Location: If you allow location access, Jewish Clock uses it to calculate local zmanim. The developer does not collect your precise location.
  • Photo backgrounds: If you choose a photo album or image, it is used on your device as a clock background. The developer does not receive the selected photos.
  • Low-light mode: This optional feature is off until you explicitly enable it and allow camera access. While the app is visible, it samples ambient brightness. Camera images are not saved or sent to the developer.
  • Alarms and preferences: Alarm schedules, display choices, and related preferences are used to provide the app's features. Some preferences may use your private Apple iCloud account when you enable compatible sharing.
  • Pocket Luach Deluxe context: If you separately configure sharing with Pocket Luach Deluxe, Jewish Clock reads available calendar context. It does not edit Pocket Luach Deluxe data.

External headline and stock-quote feeds

Jewish Clock on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro does not offer Top News or make external headline or stock-quote requests, and it has no unrestricted web browser.

Contact Support

Jewish Clock includes an optional support feature. When you deliberately start a support conversation, the service may process:

  • your name;
  • your support message and other content you choose to send;
  • an optional profile image, photo, video, or file attachment;
  • an opaque support user identifier; and
  • limited troubleshooting diagnostics.

This information is linked to your support conversation and used only for app functionality and customer support. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

Support content is transmitted through Apple's CloudKit services using HelpKit's protected support workflow. The developer retains it only as needed to answer the request, maintain the support history, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion through Contact Support in the app or by emailing haim@tebeka.com.

Apple services

Apple may process information under Apple's own privacy policy when you use the App Store, iCloud, CloudKit, Photos, location permission, camera permission, or other operating-system services. You can withdraw Camera, Photos, or Location permission at any time in Apple device Settings. You can remove locally stored preferences by deleting the app; iCloud information is controlled through your Apple account and compatible app settings.

Children

Jewish Clock is a general-audience utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. A child should use Contact Support only with a parent or guardian.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, the updated date and policy will be published before the new behavior is released. For privacy questions or deletion requests, use Contact Support inside Jewish Clock, visit Jewish Clock Support, or email haim@tebeka.com.
